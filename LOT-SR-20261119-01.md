## v48 ADDITIONS — THE BODY ELECTRIC

### Design Philosophy

The Body Electric is the somatic vocabulary: the body not as background, not as obstacle, but as intelligent signal processor. Chi, prana, qi, ki — four traditions converging on one insight: there is an animating current in living tissue. Walt Whitman named it in 1855. The practice attends to it.

This engine completes the nature-somatic arc: v37 brought the outer world (forest, soil, emergence); v38 brings the inner world (posture, breath, flow). Together they frame the practitioner as both organic being and information system — the core RPG/Sci-Fi self-care identity.
