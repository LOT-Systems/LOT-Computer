## DESIGN NOTES — THE ROGUE TERMINAL

### Why Roguelikes?

The roguelike genre has been a hidden metaphor for the LOT practice from the start:

1. **Permadeath = This moment matters.** There is no "I'll make up for it tomorrow."
   Every day is the only version of that day.

2. **Procedural generation = No two days are the same.** You cannot memorize your
   way through the practice. You can only build skills that work in any configuration.

3. **Loot = Noticing good things.** "Found treasure" is the reward-seeking frame that
   makes the practice fun. The roguelike user finds something good and names it.

4. **Boss rooms = Named challenges.** The roguelike vocabulary gives users permission
   to call the hard thing a boss. This is therapeutic permission-granting through play.

5. **Character class = Role clarity.** Choosing your class is choosing how you approach
   the day. The "class_chosen" badge rewards self-knowledge about one's mode.

### Word Turn Engine Philosophy

Each Word Turn engine (v1–v23) adds 12 vocabulary patterns that users can trigger
naturally through journaling. The LOT system detects these keywords and awards badges,
creating a positive reinforcement loop that makes writing more in specific areas
feel rewarding. The Rogue Terminal vocabulary is particularly aligned with:
- Action and agency language
- Challenge-framing language
- Recovery and health language
- Identity and role language

## LOT ARCADE EASTER EGG HIGHLIGHTS

The v33 session introduces three particularly compelling Easter egg moments:

**ZORK WORD** `∿·□·∿` — When a user writes "it is pitch dark" in their journal,
they unlock a hidden badge referencing the 1980 Zork text adventure. The unlock
message: *"The grue lives in the dark. The journal is the torch. Keep writing."*
This is designed to feel like a genuine surprise discovery.

**ULTIMA CODE** `∞·◈·∞` — Writing "virtue" triggers a MYTHIC hidden badge
referencing Ultima IV (1985), the first RPG where the goal was not to defeat a
villain but to embody virtues. The unlock message: *"Self-care as the highest quest."*
This is the deepest easter egg — philosophical and literary simultaneously.

**MARATHON RUN** `≋·∞·≋` — A 7-day streak badge that fires automatically, framed
as a roguelike "marathon run" rather than a generic streak. The reframing makes
the same underlying mechanic feel new and thematically consistent.
