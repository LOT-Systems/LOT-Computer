## 3. NEW BADGE ENGINE — v33: THE STOIC TERMINAL

### Theme Philosophy

Stoicism is the original self-care operating system. Marcus Aurelius wrote the
*Meditations* in Greek, secretly, as private notes to himself — never published, never
intended for an audience. It is the most intimate self-improvement document in history.
Epictetus was born enslaved and became the most influential teacher of his generation.
Seneca wrote about dying well while living in Caesar's most dangerous court.

This is not academic philosophy. It is a practice vocabulary for resilience, presence,
and the management of what is and is not within one's control. The LOT terminal is the
modern equivalent of the Stoic notebook.
