**Compression ladder:** daily signals → weekly digest → **Month Card**
(≤ 90 words + affirmation + word) → quarterly Chapter → Year Story → one
sentence → one word → the standing context of LOT® AI. Every Card shows its
ratio as a footer, e.g. `157 signals → 1 paragraph`, so the operator can see
the compression.

**Seal mechanic:** a month seals when 30 Usership days have elapsed AND the
four-signal quorum is met (Entries / Morning / Care / Memory). Missed months
stay *Open* and seal later; nothing is ever re-locked. There is one grace token
per quarter. The minimum path is 115 entries · 160 mornings · 228 care · 137
answers.

**Codex:** USERSHIP I · SEED … USERSHIP XII · LOT® AI, CHAPTER I–IV, THE YEAR,
UNBROKEN, LATE BLOOM, FIRST LIGHT, CARE · 100/365, LOG · 100/365.

**Machiavelli:** 12 monthly words (Exile → Virtù) and a Year Sentence are
proposed for the demo payload.
