## 6. LEXICON (new terms)

- **Breadth**: how many of the 7 trauma dimensions are present at once. A wide spread raises the risk level even when each dimension is only faintly present.
- **Floor rule**: QI·46 can raise the shared detector's verdict but never lower it.
- **Calibrate me**: the opt-in. Without it, nothing is uploaded.
- **Risk signals**: labels that explain a risk level without quoting the person.
