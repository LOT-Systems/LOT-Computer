## 5. DECISIONS

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Negation labels, never lowers** | A wrong downgrade could put a person in crisis in front of the experimental voice. A wrong upgrade only sends them the standard prompt, plus a reply that must bridge to a human. The costs are not symmetric |
| D2 | Close false negatives first | Missing `dont`, `suicidal` and `can't go on` was the real gap. Negation was a false-positive problem, and false positives already fail safe |
| D3 | Negator must be the nearest word (fillers skipped), within the same clause | It stops "I'm **not** okay, I want to die" from being labelled negated |
| D4 | Accept idiom false positives (`can't go on`, `suicide` in an article) | They fail safe. The person gets the standard path for that window. This is logged as F11 to watch |
| D5 | Scan per field and per clause | It removes cross-log stitching and makes the negation check local |

## 7. LEXICON (new terms)

- **Fold**: normalizing a spelling variant to one form (`dont`, `do not` → `don't`).
- **Negated hit**: an ideation phrase whose nearest preceding word is a negator. It is a label, not a lower level.
- **Stitch**: a false match made by joining two separate texts. It is now impossible.

## 8. DOCTRINE (distilled)
