## 5. DECISIONS

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | **Label, not level.** `language:uncovered` does not raise risk | Writing in Russian is not a risk factor. Raising the level would make COSMO® demand a human bridge from a person who may be fine, and would skew the `byRisk` read-out |
| D2 | **Skip the surface instead** (gate 5) | Unknown risk takes the same safe route as elevated risk: the standard prompt. The cost is one person missing the calibrated voice, and that is the cheap side |
| D3 | One uncovered field is enough | Ideation can be a single line. Counting a share of fields would let one Russian note slip through among English check-ins |
| D4 | Function-word vote, not a language-detection library | No new dependency. It is explainable in one line and runs per field in microseconds. English loanwords and names do not trip it |
| D5 | No machine-translated safety phrases | A wrong or missing phrase in a safety list gives false confidence. Native-speaker lists only |

## 7. LEXICON (new terms)

- **Coverage**: whether the safety scan can read the language a field is written in.
- **Uncovered**: a field the English-only scan cannot read. It means unknown risk, not no risk.
- **Function-word vote**: comparing common small words (*the, and, que, ich, je*) to guess a field's language without a library.
