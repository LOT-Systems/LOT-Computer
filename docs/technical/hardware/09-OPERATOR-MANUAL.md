# LOT PLATE — Operator Manual (DRAFT)

REV A · 2026-10-31 · Draft for the future product. Behavior described is the design target, not shipped.

## 1. Contents
LOT PLATE · Qi dock · USB-C cable (dock) · this manual.

## 2. Controls
Front: screen (off until notified), camera (eye), button COPY. Back: polished steel, black glass disc = charging window.

## 3. First use
1. Place PLATE on dock, glass down. Charge to 100 %.
2. lot-systems.com → Settings → Devices → Add device. Note the 6-digit code.
3. Hold COPY 5 s. Join the "LOT-PLATE-xxxx" network, enter Wi-Fi and the code. Screen shows "PAIRED."

## 4. Daily use
Notification arrives: screen lights, short vibration. Read it. Press COPY to record it; the Log tab shows `device_copy`. No press: the message clears after 15 min. Silence is clean; the plate says nothing without cause.

## 5. Camera
Captures only when you request it from the site. LED lights on every capture; each capture is logged.

## 6. Care
Polished steel scratches: use a microfibre cloth. Not waterproof (drip-resistant only). Charge at 0–35 °C. Do not use damaged battery/hardware; do not open.

## 7. Reset / remove
Settings → Devices → Revoke: the plate wipes its token. Hold COPY 15 s for factory reset.
