## In the box
LOT Computer · wireless charging pad · USB-C cable · this manual.

## Know your device
- **Polished steel side** — the back. Rest it on the charging pad.
- **Screen side** — shows notifications from lot-systems.com.
- **Button** — the one control.
- **Camera** — lights an indicator whenever it is active.

## Set up
1. In lot-systems.com open Settings and choose *Add device*; note the code.
2. Connect the device to your computer and run `lotctl provision`.
3. Place the device on the pad; it wakes and shows "Paired".

## Everyday use
| Action | Result |
|---|---|
| Notification appears | read it; it clears itself after one minute |
| Press once | **Copy** — the message is saved to your Log tab |
| Press and hold | dismiss |
| Double press | take a photo (if enabled) |

## Charging
Place the steel side on a Qi pad. Charging starts automatically.

## Care and safety
Do not open the body. Do not charge if the device is swollen or hot. Keep away
from water until an IP rating has been tested (none is claimed yet). Camera:
only photograph with consent.

## Privacy
You can revoke a device in Settings; it then loses access. Sensor and button
events are stored as Log entries you can read and delete.
