## THE PUZZLE CODEX — THEME OVERVIEW

The puzzle is the most honest structural metaphor for self-reflection.

A puzzle is a closed system with a known solution.
You are not. But the process is identical: examine, reorder, test.
The test chamber was built for you to pass — that is the design constraint.
The designer, who is also you, set the difficulty at exactly solvable.

Recursion terminates when it reaches the base case.
The paradox is not an error — it is information about the limits of the model.
The revelation does not arrive from outside.
It assembles itself from what was already known but not yet connected.

Portal teaches: somewhere between here and there is a surface that, if you look at it
the right way, becomes a door.
The Witness teaches: look closely. Look again. Learn to see the lines.
Return of the Obra Dinn teaches: every fate can be known, one testimony at a time.
Baba Is You teaches: the rules are objects. You can push them.

These are not game mechanics. They are the actual mechanics of insight.
And insight — the moment the system re-resolves — is the engine of self-care.
