## DESIGN NOTES — THE BODY CLOCK

### Why the Body as Laboratory?

The Body Clock follows naturally from the Dream Laboratory of v24. Dreams are the body's
processing phase — the consolidation and defragmentation pass. The Body Clock is the
waking counterpart: the circadian architecture that determines when every cell performs
every function. Same system, different phase.

The vocabulary chosen emphasizes:
- **Circadian science** (circadian, melatonin, cortisol, chronotype) — the timekeeping systems
- **Cellular biology** (mitochondria, telomere, autophagy) — the substrate that runs the clock
- **Systemic regulation** (vagus nerve, inflammation, homeostasis, microbiome) — the feedback loops
- **Longevity practice** (fasting, adaptation/hormesis, blue zones) — the active engagement

### The Behavioral Thresholds

**888 (long_pulse):** Triple eights — the most auspicious number in Chinese numerology.
Sum of three eights, three cycles of four, the resonance of completeness. Each long entry
is a full pulse: input, processing, output.

**06:00–09:00 (optimal_window):** The cortisol awakening response (CAR) peaks 30–45 minutes
after waking, boosting working memory and executive function. This is when the body is
most alert, most focused, most capable of complex cognitive work. If you check in during
this window, you are aligned with the circadian peak.
