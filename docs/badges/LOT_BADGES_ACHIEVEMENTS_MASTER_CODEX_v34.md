## BADGE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY — v34 NOTES

### Why Dreams?

The sleep laboratory is the most important room in the history of consciousness
science. Aserinsky and Kleitman discovered REM in 1953. LaBerge proved the
subjective report of a lucid dream with objective EEG data in 1980. Freud
proposed the dream as the royal road to the unconscious in 1899. Jung spent
16 years recording his own dream images in the Red Book.

The Dream Laboratory badges reward engagement with the science and art of
the sleeping mind — an underutilized practice in self-care systems that
focus entirely on the waking hours.

```

                     signals in an REM sleep lab. The WILD technique.
morpheus_signal    — The god of dreams. Son of Hypnos. Grandson of Nyx.
```

### The 777 Easter Egg (long_dream)

The `long_dream` badge requires 777 characters. Seven is the number of notes
in a scale, days in a week, chakras in the body. Three sevens: 777. The full
harmonic resonance. The number of completion at a higher octave.

### The 666 Easter Egg (long_spell)

The `long_spell` badge (v33) requires 666 characters. 666 is the magic square
of the sun in Kabbalah — the sum of all numbers 1 through 36. It represents
the full solar force, the visible world at maximum intensity.
