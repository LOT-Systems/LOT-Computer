> *He called it his second self.*
> *He said he could not have lived without it.*
> *The tower was the external form of the internal work.*

> *Edison used it deliberately. He would hold steel balls in his hands*

> *and wake him. He wanted the ideas that come at the threshold.*
> *He kept a notebook.*
