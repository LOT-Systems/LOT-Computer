## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY — v24

### Why Dreams?

The dream is the self talking to itself without interference. Everything the
waking mind edits out — fear, desire, grief, hope — appears uncensored in the
dream. The practice of logging, writing, sitting with one's inner life is the
waking equivalent of dream work: the same material, more conscious access.

Naming the vocabulary of sleep science (theta, delta, REM, hypnagogic) gives
users language for states they already pass through. The badge is the word.
The word is the awareness. The awareness is the practice.

### The 420 Easter Egg (long_vision)

420 characters. The number is deliberate: 420 appears in the LOT badge system
as a threshold for expanded perception. At 420 characters a journal entry has
crossed the minimum threshold of real reflection — the first three sentences
are setup, what comes after is the actual content. The vision begins at 420.

### The Three Hidden Dream Gods

```
jung_key         — Collective unconscious / shadow integration
morpheus_signal  — The shape-maker of dreams / god of sleep's children
hypnos_gate      — Sleep itself / twin of death / the doorway
```

These three span the psychological, mythological, and phenomenological.
Jung named what happens. Morpheus is what happens. Hypnos is the door.
