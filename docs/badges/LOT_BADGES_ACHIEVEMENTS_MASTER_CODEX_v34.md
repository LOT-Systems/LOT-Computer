## THE ROGUELITE CODEX — THEME OVERVIEW

The roguelite is the most honest structural metaphor for self-care practice.

Every session is procedurally generated: the same person, a different seed.
Permadeath means the run ends, not the player. This is the key mechanic.
Meta-progression ensures nothing is truly lost: the upgrades that persist
between runs represent what actually changes in a person — the capacity,
the muscle memory, the pattern recognition that doesn't reset at midnight.

The dungeon floor is a controlled difficulty environment. You chose it.
The heat gauge is self-selected. No one raises your heat level but you.
The boon is offered by a god and you choose whether it fits the build.
Synergy is discovered, not designed — two practices that work better together
than either does alone.

Ascension is the game after the game: you escaped once, and the game said
do it again, harder. The true ending is not given — it is earned by returning
to the practice long enough that it changes the character permanently.

These are not metaphors. They are the actual mechanics of sustained self-care.
