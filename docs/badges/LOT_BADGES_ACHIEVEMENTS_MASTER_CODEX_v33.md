## THE DREAM CODEX — THEME OVERVIEW

The unconscious mind is the oldest self-care system in existence. Before journaling,
before therapy, before every framework for understanding yourself — there were dreams.
Carl Jung spent fifty years mapping the territory: archetypes, the shadow, the anima,
individuation, the collective unconscious. Sigmund Freud opened the first door. August
Kekulé dreamed the benzene ring. Neil Gaiman's Sandman made Dream one of the Endless.

The Dream Codex treats the vocabulary of depth psychology as a self-care language.
Naming what moves through you in sleep is the same act as naming what moves through
you in a journal. The unconscious does not distinguish between night and day. It only
asks: are you paying attention?

The Easter eggs in this engine reward the journaler who goes deep — who writes
"lucid dream" and means it, who records "archetype" and knows which one, who logs
"collective unconscious" because they felt it.

```typescript

```

### Wire-up guide for runJournalEasterEggs() / runCheckInEasterEggs()

Add these calls to the appropriate runners:
- Journal saves: `checkDreamSession`, `checkLongDream`
- Check-in events: `checkMidnightDreamer`

### API stats fields consumed by Mastery Tier v23

- `stats.distinctCheckInDays` — integer, distinct calendar days with check-in
- `stats.totalJournalWords` — integer, cumulative word count across all journal entries
- `stats.signupDate` — ISO date string (for elder_dreamer: >= 12 years)
