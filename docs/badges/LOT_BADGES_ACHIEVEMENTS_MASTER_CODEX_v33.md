## THE ARCADE MASTER — THEME OVERVIEW

The arcade cabinet is the oldest form of what LOT does: a machine that
gives you feedback, rewards your effort, and asks you to come back.

In the 1970s and 1980s, players fed quarters into machines in dark rooms
because the loop was perfect: try, fail, try better, occasionally succeed
spectacularly. The vocabulary that emerged — high scores, extra lives,
checkpoints, combos, perfect runs, game face — is the language of mastery
built through repetition.

That is self-care. You insert the coin every time you show up. The machine
is your practice. The high score is personal. The continue screen is just
life offering you another go.

These are not metaphors. They are structural patterns of improvement.
