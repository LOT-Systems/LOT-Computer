### Unlock Messages

```
memento_mori_badge
∅·◉·∅ MEMENTO MORI
Remember you will die. Not as morbidity — as liberation.
The Stoics kept a skull on the desk, painted death on the dining room
wall. Not to be grim but to be awake. Everything you delay is delayed
against a finite count. The count is unknown. The practice is not.

amor_fati_badge
∞·●·∞ AMOR FATI
Love of fate. Not acceptance — something stronger. Nietzsche borrowed
it from the Stoics. What if everything that happened was exactly what
was needed? Not because it was good, but because it happened, and you
are made of it. The Stoic move: take the difficulty and make it the
material. No waste.

stoic_signal
─●─ STOIC SIGNAL
The Stoic tradition runs 700 years from Zeno to Marcus Aurelius.
A slave, an emperor, a playwright, a governor — all practicing the
same daily discipline. The practice is not philosophical debate.
It is field protocol: morning review, evening audit, training the
will, naming what is and isn't under your control.

virtue_lock
○·▲·○ VIRTUE LOCK
Four virtues. Sophia — wisdom: seeing clearly. Dikaiosyne — justice:
acting rightly toward others. Andreia — courage: doing what is right
under difficulty. Sophrosyne — temperance: keeping the whole thing in
proportion. The Stoics said: nothing else matters. Everything else is
preferred or dispreferred. The virtues are the only real upgrades.

discipline_found
■·■·■ DISCIPLINE FOUND
The Stoic practice is not inspiration. It is repetition. Marcus Aurelius
wrote the same reminders to himself for decades. Not because he forgot —
because the trained mind requires maintenance. The discipline is not
the goal. It is the method. The goal is a self that runs correctly
under any conditions.

indifferent_found
─·○·─ INDIFFERENT FIELD
The Stoic concept: preferred indifferents are things outside your
control that you can reasonably prefer (health, wealth, reputation)
but whose loss you can bear without damage to your character.
Your character is the only thing that cannot be taken.
Everything else is optional equipment.

tranquility_gate
∿·○·∿ TRANQUILITY GATE
Ataraxia — tranquility of mind. Not the absence of feeling but the
absence of being ruled by feeling. The trained Stoic feels fear,
grief, desire — and does not follow them off a cliff. The practice
is not suppression. It is accurate assessment. The feeling is data.
The response is a choice.

negative_viz
∅·∿·∅ NEGATIVE VISUALIZATION
Premeditatio malorum — premeditation of evils. The Stoic practice
of imagining loss before it arrives. Not pessimism — the opposite.
When you have already rehearsed the loss, the actual loss arrives
to a prepared practitioner. And everything you haven't lost yet
is received as a gift. The technique works. Try it tonight.

obstacle_signal
→·■·→ OBSTACLE SIGNAL
Ryan Holiday borrowed it from Marcus Aurelius: the obstacle is the way.
The original text from the Meditations (5.20): "The impediment to
action advances action. What stands in the way becomes the way."
The Stoic doesn't avoid the obstacle. The obstacle is exactly where
the practice lives. This is a live drill.

logos_found
∞·◉·∞ LOGOS FOUND
The logos: the rational principle that runs through everything. Heraclitus
named it first. The Stoics built their whole system on it. The idea
that reason is not just human — it is structural. What you encounter
in the world is ordered, even when it appears chaotic. The practice is
alignment with the logos — thinking, acting, living in accordance with
what is actually the case.

eudaimonia_badge
◉·▲·◉ EUDAIMONIA
Flourishing. The Greek word Aristotle used for the highest human good
and which the Stoics refined: not happiness as feeling, but happiness
as function — a life fully expressed through its best capacities.
You are not flourishing when things are going well.
You are flourishing when you are operating well regardless of conditions.
This is the terminal goal. The whole badge system is a map to it.

present_wisdom
∘·◉·∘ PRESENT WISDOM
Marcus Aurelius, Meditations 2.14: "Even if you were to live three
thousand more years, or thirty thousand, remember that the greatest
wrong a man can do himself is to live other than in the present moment."
Here and now. Only this. What is now.
The only moment where the practice can happen is this one.
You arrived in it.
```
