## BADGE DESIGN PHILOSOPHY — v43

```
THE DREAM ENGINE PRINCIPLE

"Eight hours of every day are spent in the other system.
 LOT tracks sixteen. The dream engine tracks the other eight."

LOT's self-care RPG runs in two modes:
  WAKING MODE — check-ins, journals, memory, habits
  DREAM MODE  — sleep tracking, dream logging, hypnagogic work

The Dream Weaver engine creates incentives for:
  · Keeping a dream journal (dream_archive_badge, dream_logger)
  · Naming sleep difficulties (sleep_debt_found, somnolent_found)
  · Celebrating weird phenomenology (hypnagogic_badge, astral_found)
  · Engaging sleep science (sleep_cycle_signal, waking_dream_found)
  · Recognizing the collective dimension (collective_dream_found)

The nightmare is a boss fight. The lucid dream is a rare drop.
The dream diary is the dungeon log.
The unconscious is the game's deepest dungeon.
```
