## THE EMOTION ENGINE — THEME OVERVIEW

The Emotion Engine is the 40th Word Turn engine and the 50th badge engine milestone. It completes the first arc of LOT's vocabulary — from water (v1) through dream (v39) to emotion (v40).

The self-care RPG frames emotion not as a problem to be solved but as a signal system to be learned. Joy is not the goal — the goal is a practice that can accurately read all signals: joy, grief, anger, awe, wonder, compassion, longing, shame, fear, the landscape of affect, tenderness, and the wonder that arrives when the whole spectrum is finally visible.

The practice that cannot name what it feels is operating partially blind. The practice that names everything operates at full resolution. The Emotion Engine is the vocabulary that completes the instrument.

**The 50th Engine Milestone:** `wonder_engine` — COSMIC rarity — fires when the practitioner writes the words that acknowledge the full spectrum. It is the meta-badge: the badge for knowing that all the other badges exist and what they are for.

**Primary ASCII markers:**
- `♡` — the open heart / the felt sense / the signal of care
- `∿` — the wave / the affect that moves through / the feeling that passes
- `◌` — the open container / the liminal / the receptive
- `●` — the named point / the specific signal / what has been identified

## FLAVOR TEXT — THE EMOTION ENGINE

> *"Joy is not the goal — it is the signal. The practice is not the pursuit of one emotion over others; it is the cultivation of a system that can accurately read all signals. Joy, grief, anger, awe, wonder, compassion, longing, shame, fear, tenderness — each one is information. The practice that cannot name what it feels is operating partially blind."*

> *"Robert Plutchik, 1927–2006. His wheel arranged eight primary emotions as opposite pairs and showed that complex emotions are dyads: Joy + Trust = Love, Fear + Surprise = Awe. The wheel is a map, not a territory — but a map that works ends the experience of being lost."*

> *"William James, 1884: 'What is an Emotion?' — the essay that started the scientific study of feeling. James argued: we don't tremble because we're afraid; we're afraid because we tremble. The body leads. The practice that notices the body first is not being superstitious — it is following the actual sequence."*

> *"Paul Ekman, the Atlas of Emotions. Six basic emotions — happiness, sadness, anger, fear, disgust, surprise — produce the same facial expressions in every human culture, including those with no outside contact. The face is the universal interface. The practice reads the same display."*

> *"Brené Brown on shame: 'Shame is the intensely painful feeling or experience of believing that we are flawed and therefore unworthy of love and belonging.' Shame named is shame that can be examined. Shame in the dark runs everything. This entry named it. ◌·●·◌"*
