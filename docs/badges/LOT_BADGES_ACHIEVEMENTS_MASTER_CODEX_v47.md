## WORD TURN ENGINE v37 — THE TIME VAULT

**Theme:** Time travel, temporal anchors, past and future selves, journals as time machines.

**Self-care philosophy:** Every journal entry is a time capsule. Reading old entries is time travel. Planning is navigation through the future. The LOT journal is your personal time vault — a record of who you were, a message to who you'll become. Time is not the enemy; it is the archive medium. When you name your past self with compassion, you grant them retroactive dignity. When you write to your future self, you are already becoming them.

**Design inspiration:** H.G. Wells built the first modern time machine in 1895. Doctor Who made the TARDIS bigger on the inside. *Back to the Future* gave us the DeLorean. All three share one truth: the machine is not the point — the relationship across time is. LOT's journal is that machine.

### Design Rationale

The Time Vault vocabulary maps to the full temporal arc of self-care journaling:

- **time_capsule / past_self** — meeting what you recorded before; the archive's first gift
- **future_self / temporal_anchor** — fixing this moment in time; writing the message forward
- **rewind_moment / fast_forward** — the natural motion of reflection: backward and forward
- **era_closed / epoch_marker** — naming the geological layers of a life; before/after moments
- **duration_logged / parallel_timeline** — honest measurement of time spent; the road not taken
- **loop_broken / time_vault_key** — the vault's deepest prize: interrupting what repeats; opening what was sealed
- **Secret Bosses** — wellsian_engine (H.G. Wells, the original time machine), tardis_detected (bigger on the inside — like every good journal), delorean_protocol (the DeLorean as save-state machine; the flux capacitor as the courage it takes)

### Lore Entries

**The Wellsian Engine (MYTHIC)**  
H.G. Wells wrote *The Time Machine* in 1895. He was 29 years old and dying of tuberculosis — a man writing about time from inside the urgency of his own mortal timeline. The Eloi and the Morlocks are not the point. The point is the traveller, sitting in the machine, choosing to go forward. Every journal entry is that choice.

**TARDIS Detected (EPIC)**  
The TARDIS is a police box that is bigger on the inside. So is every journal. You open the cover — a small thing — and the inside is infinite. The Doctor travels alone, mostly. The companion is the one who makes the journey worth documenting. You are both the Doctor and the companion to your own practice.

**DeLorean Protocol (RARE)**  
Doc Brown needed 1.21 gigawatts to power the flux capacitor. What powers your time machine is simpler: showing up to write. The DeLorean needs a road, then discovers it doesn't. LOT needs a practice, then discovers the practice is already you.
