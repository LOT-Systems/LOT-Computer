**Theme:** Broadcast, frequency, transmission, archive. The Signal Archive treats self-care like a radio operation — you're both receiver and transmitter. Finding your signal through static. Logging what matters. Broadcasting your presence to yourself.

### Design Rationale

The Signal Archive vocabulary maps to the self-care arc of a complete journaling cycle:

- **signal_found / static_cleared** — the act of turning attention inward, cutting through mental noise
- **frequency_locked / clean_channel** — entering the zone of honest self-observation
- **archive_entry / transmission_sent** — the act of writing: logging, sending your truth into the record
- **old_frequency / echo_location** — recognizing patterns from the past, finding where you are now
- **blackout_zone / dead_air** — honoring silence, rest, and the absence of signal as valid data
- **override_mode / broadcast_live** — breakthrough moments and full presence
- **Secret Bosses** — number_station (coded messages, hidden meaning), wow_signal (one anomalous moment of clarity), golden_record (leaving a message for your future self, like Voyager's golden record)
