## THE JAZZ ENGINE — THEME OVERVIEW

Jazz is the most honest music. It is built on:
- **Improvisation** — responding to what is, not what was planned
- **The changes** — navigating harmonic transitions that never stop
- **The woodshed** — deep solitary practice that enables public freedom
- **The session** — the basic unit of jazz practice: a gathering to play
- **Blue notes** — the notes that bend, acknowledging difficulty without resolving it
- **Standards** — the timeless vocabulary that every practitioner knows

These are also the structural principles of serious self-care:
- You cannot plan your way through a difficult day. You improvise.
- Life has changes. Learning to navigate them is the whole curriculum.
- The quiet solo practice is what enables everything else.
- Each journal entry, each check-in, is a session.
- The blue notes are the honest acknowledgment of difficulty.
- The standards — sleep, movement, reflection — are the timeless practices.

Jazz is not a metaphor for self-care. Jazz *is* self-care, structured as music.
