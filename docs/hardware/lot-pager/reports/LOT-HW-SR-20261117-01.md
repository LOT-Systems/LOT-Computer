## 04 · KEY FINDINGS (analysis)

1. **Stainless steel blocks Qi and Wi-Fi (R1, high).** Eddy currents in a conductive back turn the charging field into heat, and metal shields 2.4 GHz. Decision: the coil and antenna sit under the front glass and the unit charges face-down, so the polished side stays up. Fallback: pogo puck.
2. **5 mm is achievable, but the camera is the tall pole (R2, high).** The plan uses a PCB cut-out and puts the battery beside the camera. Real module z-heights are not published by suppliers, so drawings must be requested.
3. **Certification cost (R3).** A chip-down radio for 5 mm means full FCC/CE intentional-radiator testing (about $10–20k). Plan: build EVT on the pre-certified module, then decide at DVT.
4. **Cost.** About **$111 per unit** variable, **≈ $16k** for the 100-unit programme without certification and **≈ $26–36k** with it.
5. **Battery.** About 6.4 mAh/day on 100 mAh gives **≈ 15 days** per charge (estimate; measure on EVT).
