## In the box

- One tile.

## The tile

The polished side faces down. The screen faces you.

```
 ┌──────────────────────────┐
 │ LOT                    ◉ │  ◉ camera, with a light beside it while it is on
 │ ──────────────────────── │
 │ Coffee time!             │  the line from lot-systems.com
 │                          │
 │ Synced.             [■]  │  [■] COPY
 └──────────────────────────┘
```

## Charge

Place the tile polished side down on the puck. It centres itself.
The puck light is on while the tile charges and off when the tile is full, after about 2 hours.
Other wireless pads may not charge the tile. Use the LOT puck.

## Pair (first time)

1. Hold COPY for 5 seconds. The screen shows `Pair.`
2. Open lot-systems.com on your phone, then go to Settings, then Devices, then Add tile.
3. Choose your Wi-Fi network. The tile shows a code, for example `K7Q-2MX`. Enter it.
4. The screen shows `Synced.` The tile is now yours.

## Use

- **Read:** a new line arrives with one soft buzz. The line stays on the screen, and the
  screen draws no power while it shows a still image.
- **COPY:** press once. The line, the time, and the tile's own weather reading go into
  your Log. The screen shows `Copied.`
- **Photo:** hold COPY for about 1 second. The camera light turns on. Your Log receives a short
  description of the photo. The photo is not kept on the tile.
- **Quiet hours:** no buzz between 22:00 and 07:00.

## Screen words

| Word | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| `Synced.` | The line is current |
| `Copied.` | It is in your Log |
| `Not sent. Retry.` | There was no network. Press COPY again |
| `Offline.` | Wi-Fi was not found. The last line is kept |
| `Low.` | Under 10 %. Put the tile on the puck |
| `Quiet.` | Nothing to say. That is fine |

## Care

- Wipe the polished side with a dry microfibre cloth.
- The tile resists splashes but is not for swimming.
- Do not cover the small hole near the edge. The weather sensor breathes through it.
- Battery: lithium polymer. Do not heat, puncture or put it in household waste.

## Remove

On lot-systems.com go to Settings, then Devices, then Revoke. To reset the tile, hold COPY for 15 seconds.
