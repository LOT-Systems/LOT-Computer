# LOT PAGER — USER MANUAL

*A quiet square of steel that tells you, now and then, what your day needs.*

## In the box

- LOT PAGER (40 × 40 × 5 mm, stainless steel)
- Wireless charging pad
- This manual

## Meet your pager

- **Polished side** — the mirror face. Leave it up.
- **Window side** — the screen, the camera, and one button: **COPY**.

## 1 · Charge

Place the pager **window side down** on the charging pad. A full charge takes
about 2–3 hours and lasts about two weeks.

## 2 · Connect to LOT

1. Sign in at **lot-systems.com** and open **Settings → Devices → Pair**.
   *(Edition 0.1 note: the Devices screen ships in session S2; until then pairing is done with `lot-pager-cli pair`.)*
2. Keep the code that appears; it is shown only once.
3. Connect the pager to a computer with the provisioning cable, then follow the
   on-screen steps (or run `lot-pager-cli provision`). You will enter your Wi-Fi name, password and the code.
4. The screen shows **LOT · NEW** when the first message arrives.

## 3 · Everyday use

- Messages arrive on their own: *Coffee time!*, *Stretch. Two minutes. Now.*
- **NEW** means a fresh message. **+2** means two more are waiting; they follow within a minute.
- Press **COPY** to save the message to your **Log** on lot-systems.com. The screen shows **COPIED**.
- At night the pager checks in less often to save battery.

## 4 · What the screen tells you

| You see | It means |
|---------|----------|
| SETUP | Wi-Fi not set yet |
| OFF | No connection right now; it will try again by itself |
| PAIR | The pager was removed from your account; pair it again |
| COPIED | Saved to your Log |

## 5 · Care

- Wipe the polished side with a microfibre cloth. No abrasives.
- Water-resistant to splashes, not to submersion.
- Do not charge on metal surfaces or with coins/keys between pager and pad.
- Operating temperature 0–40 °C.

## 6 · Safety & compliance

Contains a lithium-polymer battery. Do not open, puncture or heat. Recycle as electronic waste.
Regulatory marks (FCC / CE) are printed on the inside of the back plate after certification.
