# LOT Computer — User Manual

## What It Is

LOT Computer is a small stainless steel object that shows you one line at a
time — a notification generated by your LOT Memory Engine and Quantum
Operating System — and lets you log a moment back to your Log tab with a
single button. It is not a screen you check. It is an object that tells you
when to look.

## In the Box

- 1× LOT Computer (40mm × 40mm stainless tile)
- 1× Wireless charging dock
- This manual (Quick Start card also included, printed)

## Setup

1. Place LOT Computer on the charging dock. The display will show `Pair me`.
2. Open lot-systems.com on your phone or computer, sign in, and go to
   **Settings → Devices → Add Device**.
3. Enter the device ID printed on the back of your unit, or scan its code.
4. A 6-digit code appears on the LOT Computer's display. Enter it on the
   web page. Pairing completes in a few seconds.
5. Leave it on the dock overnight the first time to reach a full charge.

## Daily Use

- **The screen:** shows one short message at a time — "Coffee time.",
  a mode change from your Quantum Operating System, or a pattern the
  Memory Engine noticed. It refreshes on its own; you never need to press
  anything to see the next one.
- **The button ("Copy"):** press it whenever a moment is worth logging —
  the display briefly shows "Logged." and the moment appears in your Log
  tab on lot-systems.com, timestamped to the second you pressed it.
- **Charging:** set it back on its dock when convenient. Wireless only —
  there is no port on the device to align or wear out.

## Care — Stainless Steel Body

- The mirror face (front) shows fingerprints by design — it's polished
  stainless, the same material as a watch case. Wipe with a soft
  microfiber cloth; avoid abrasive cleaners, which will dull the mirror
  finish permanently.
- The back face is bead-blasted and hides fingerprints better — it can be
  wiped the same way.
- The device is splash-resistant (sealed gasket at the shell seam) but is
  not designed for submersion.
- Do not attempt to open the two-part shell — there are no
  user-serviceable parts inside, and reopening it breaks the factory seal.

## Privacy

- LOT Computer's weather sensor reads ambient temperature, humidity, and
  pressure from the room it sits in — nothing else. This feeds the same
  weather-mood insight system already used by the LOT web app.
- Camera capability, on units equipped with it, is off by default and must
  be explicitly enabled in Settings before the device will ever send an
  image anywhere.
- A Copy-button press sends only a timestamp — never audio, never a
  photo, never your location.
- You can unpair the device at any time from **Settings → Devices**. An
  unpaired device shows `Pair me` and stores nothing further until paired
  again.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Try this |
|---|---|
| Display stuck on "Pair me" | Confirm Wi-Fi credentials were entered correctly during setup; re-pair from Settings → Devices |
| No new messages appearing | Check your account's Quantum Operating System mode isn't set to `maintenance` (lower message frequency by design) |
| Copy button doesn't flash "Logged." | Check the dock — a low battery can delay the confirmation flash; charge and retry |
| Device feels warm during charging | Normal for wireless charging; stop use and contact support if it feels hot to the touch |
