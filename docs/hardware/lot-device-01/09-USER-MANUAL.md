# LOT DEVICE-01 · SQUARE — User Manual

**Doc:** HW-09-MAN · **Rev:** A (draft for EVT) · **Date:** 2026-11-28

## In the box

- SQUARE, a 40 × 40 × 5 mm stainless steel device
- The LOT charging puck
- A USB-C cable
- A card with a QR code to this manual

## 1. Know your SQUARE

- **Mirror side:** polished stainless steel. Keep it face-up on the desk.
- **Working side:** the screen, the camera (top-left) and the **Copy** button (lower-right edge).

## 2. First charge

Place SQUARE **screen-side down** in the puck. The screen shows `CHARGING.`
A full charge takes about 2.5 hours.
The mirror side does not charge. If you place it mirror-down, the screen asks `FLIP ME.`

## 3. Connect to LOT

1. Hold the Copy button for 8 s. The screen shows `SETUP.`
2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network `LOT-SQUARE-xxxx` and pick your home Wi-Fi.
3. The screen shows a code, for example `PAIR K7Q-4MX`.
4. On lot-systems.com, open **Settings → Devices**, or go to **lot-systems.com/device**. Type the code there.
5. The screen shows `CONNECTED.`

## 4. Everyday use

| You see or feel | It means |
|---|---|
| One soft vibration and a new line on the screen | LOT sent a notice, for example "Coffee time!" |
| Clock · temperature · battery | No current notice |

| You do | SQUARE does |
|---|---|
| **Press** Copy | Copies the notice to your **Log** tab, with the room temperature and humidity. The screen shows `Copied.` |
| **Hold** Copy for 1.5 s | Takes one photo, after a countdown of 3-2-1, and adds it to your Log |
| **Press twice** | Dismisses the notice |

If there is no Wi-Fi, copies are saved on the device and sent later.

## 5. Care

- Clean the mirror side with a dry microfibre cloth. Don't use abrasive cleaners.
- It resists splashes, but it is **not** for swimming. Keep the small vent on the side clear.
- Charge it at 0–35 °C.

## 6. Privacy

- The camera works only when you hold the button, and it never takes photos automatically.
- Photos and notices go only to your LOT account.
- To unpair: hold Copy for 8 s, then another 8 s. Or remove the device in Settings → Devices.
