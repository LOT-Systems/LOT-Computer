# What's in the Box

| Item | Qty |
|------|-----|
| COSMO&reg; Cube (40&times;40&times;5mm, 316L stainless steel) | 1 |
| Qi wireless charging pad (80&times;80&times;6mm) | 1 |
| USB-C to USB-A cable, 1.5m, braided | 1 |
| Quick Start Guide (this document) | 1 |

No USB port on the Cube itself &mdash; it charges wirelessly only, by design. The charging pad is the only cable in the box.

# 1. First Charge

1. Plug the charging pad into any USB-C power source (5V/2A or better).
2. Place the Cube on the pad, mirror-polished side down.
3. The status LED breathes white while charging, and turns solid green at full charge.
4. First charge takes about 2.5 hours from empty.

Charge fully before first use. The Cube ships at a storage charge (~40%), not full.

# 2. Pairing to Your LOT Account

1. Press and hold the Copy button for 3 seconds. The screen shows a QR code.
2. On your phone or computer, open **lot-systems.com/devices** and sign in with your LOT Usership account.
3. Scan the QR code, or enter the 6-digit code shown beneath it.
4. The Cube's screen confirms **"Paired."** and returns to the clock face.

**Note:** The Cube only activates for accounts with a Benchmark score of Purple tier or higher. If your account has not reached Purple, the Cube will display **"Not yet eligible"** and remain in pairing mode. This is not a bug &mdash; it is the same Benchmark Arbitrage&reg; gate that governs every COSMO&reg; hardware product.

# 3. Using the Screen

The 1" square display shows one of these states at any time:

| Screen | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Clock | Default state &mdash; time, date, current temperature |
| Notification | A message from the LOT AI, e.g. "Coffee time!" |
| Charging | Battery percentage while on the charging pad |
| Logged. | Confirms a Copy button press was recorded |
| Low battery | Reminder to return the Cube to its charging pad |

Notifications appear automatically &mdash; there is nothing to open or check. The LOT AI decides when something is worth a glance, and the Cube shows it for 30 seconds before returning to the clock.

# 4. The Copy Button

The single button on the front face has one job: **log this moment.**

Press it once, and the Cube captures the temperature, humidity, light level, and orientation around you right now, and sends it to your **Log tab** on lot-systems.com, tagged **[COSMO&reg; Cube]**.

There is no confirmation screen to dismiss and nothing to type. A green LED pulse means it was recorded; a red pulse (retried automatically up to 3 times) means it will sync on the next connection.

Over time, your press pattern &mdash; when you reach for the Cube, and what the room was like when you did &mdash; becomes part of your Quantum Intent Engine signal, the same way a journal entry or mood check-in does.

# 5. Care

- **Body:** 316L marine-grade stainless steel. Wipe with a soft, dry cloth. The mirror-polished back will show fingerprints &mdash; this is expected of a #8 polish finish, not a defect.
- **Water:** IP54 splash-resistant. Not for submersion or the shower.
- **Temperature:** Operates 0&deg;C to +45&deg;C. Store between -20&deg;C and +60&deg;C.
- **Charging:** Wireless only. Do not attempt to pry open the body &mdash; there is no user-serviceable port or battery access. Contact LOT Systems for service.

# 6. Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
|---------|-----------|
| Screen stays blank | Place on charging pad for 10 minutes, then press Copy once |
| Won't pair | Confirm Benchmark tier is Purple or higher at lot-systems.com |
| No notifications arriving | Check WiFi credentials were entered correctly during pairing |
| LED pulses red repeatedly | Weak WiFi signal &mdash; move charging pad closer to your router |
