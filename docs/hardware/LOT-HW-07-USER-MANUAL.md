# LOT Pager — User Manual (draft)

## In the box
LOT Pager · wireless charging pad · USB-C cable · this card.

## Start
1. Put the pager face-down on the pad for 1 hour (charges through the screen side).
2. On lot-systems.com: Settings → Devices → **Pair** (until that page exists, use the companion tool).
3. The screen shows **Ready.**

## Use
- A notice arrives with two short buzzes, for example **Coffee time!**
- Press the button once to **Copy** it to your Log. You feel one short buzz.
- Hold the button 1.5 s to dismiss.

## Care
The polished side is bare 316L stainless steel. Wipe it with a soft cloth. Keep the pager away from keys and coins. Not waterproof.

## Privacy
The camera is off by default. A light shows whenever it is on. Version 1 sends no images.

## Safety
Lithium battery. Do not open, puncture or heat above 45 °C. Charge with a certified 5 W pad only.
