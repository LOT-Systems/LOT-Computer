# LD-01 · User Manual

**Document:** LD-01-DOC-08 · Rev A draft · 2026-11-21

## In the box

LD-01. Wireless charging pad. USB-C cable. This card.

## Know your LD-01

- **Mirror side.** Polished steel. Leave it up.
- **Window side.** Screen, camera, and the **Copy** button.

## Start

1. Place LD-01 window-side down on the pad. Charge for 1 hour.
2. Pick it up. The screen shows a six-letter code.
3. On lot-systems.com, open **Settings → Devices** and enter the code.
4. The screen shows `Paired.`

## Use

| You see | You do | What happens |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| `Coffee time.` | Nothing | It stays on screen. No light and no sound, just one soft tick |
| Something worth keeping | Press **Copy** | It goes to your **Log** tab with time and weather. The screen shows `Copied.` |
| A moment | Hold **Copy** for 2 seconds | LD-01 takes one picture. LOT writes a caption into your Log. The picture is discarded unless you choose to keep pictures |
| More waiting | Tap the case twice | Shows the next message |

The bottom line always shows temperature, humidity and pressure where you are.

## Charge

Place it window-side down on the pad. A full charge takes about 1.5 hours and lasts about a week.

## Reset

Hold **Copy** for 10 seconds. LD-01 forgets Wi-Fi and pairing.

## Care

Wipe the mirror side with a dry microfiber cloth. Avoid abrasives.
Do not open the case. Do not charge above 40 °C.

## Safety and compliance

Contains a lithium-ion battery. Contains FCC ID: (module ID). This device complies with Part 15 of the FCC rules.
