## 2 · KEY DECISIONS

1. **Qi coil under the front glass.** Steel blocks wireless charging, so the
   device charges face-down; the mirror back stays unbroken.
2. **RF exits through the glass and a 0.3 mm PEEK seam gasket.** Must be
   measured at EVT.
3. **EVT at 6.0 mm, DVT at 5.0 mm.** 5 mm with camera + battery + Qi is not
   proven by datasheets alone.
4. **MCU: ESP32-S3-PICO-1-N8R2** (7 × 7 × 1 mm SiP, Wi-Fi + BLE + camera
   interface + PSRAM). Plan B: pre-certified module if thickness allows.
5. **Screen: Sharp 1.28" memory LCD** — silver, reflective, holds its image
   with no power, which suits a pager that sleeps.
6. **Pager = poll, not push.** Deep sleep + poll every 5 min (server sets
   `next_poll_s`) → about 3 days on 90 mAh. Always-on Wi-Fi would roughly
   halve that.
7. **COPY writes a Log row** with `event: device_copy` in the existing `logs`
   table; long-press adds one camera frame.
8. **Compression happens on the server**: ≤ 64 chars, no emoji, period at the end.
