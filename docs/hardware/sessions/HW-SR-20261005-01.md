## Key findings
1. 5 mm total thickness is the main feasibility risk; recommend EVT at 7–8 mm.
2. Qi charging through stainless steel is lossy; needs front-face charging or a non-metal inlay.
3. Metal case also hurts the Wi-Fi antenna; needs an RF window.
4. Existing /api/live-message is global and unauthenticated for device use; a per-device token layer is required.
5. 100-unit run: ~$9k–18k (~$95–170/unit) excluding compliance.
