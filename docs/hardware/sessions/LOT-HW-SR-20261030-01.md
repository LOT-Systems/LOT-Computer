## Key findings
1. 5 mm thickness impossible with battery + Qi + camera + screen; ~8.5–9.5 mm.
2. Stainless blocks both Wi-Fi and Qi; body needs a dielectric window/seam and a non-metal charging face.
3. Ambient-AI doc says "no push notifications"; pager concept needs reframing.
4. 100-unit run ≈ $10–17k excluding certification; unit cost ≈ $65–90.
5. Battery life at 60 s polling ≈ 2 days; use 5 min or light-sleep.
6. "Made in the USA" line must not be used for PCBWay-built hardware without legal review.
