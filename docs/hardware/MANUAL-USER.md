# LOT® Pager — User Manual

*Draft for prototype units. Subject to change before production.*

## What it is
A small stainless-steel square that receives notifications from lot-systems.com, and sends a signal back when you press its button.

## Controls
| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Short press | **Copy** — marks the notification and writes it to your Log tab |
| Long press (2 s) | Acknowledge / dismiss |
| Hold 10 s | Pairing mode |

## Setup
1. Place the Pager on a Qi charging pad until the screen lights.
2. On lot-systems.com open Settings → Add Pager. A 6-character code appears.
3. Hold the button for 10 seconds. Choose your Wi-Fi and enter the code on the Pager.
4. The screen says "Paired."

## Daily use
Notifications appear on the screen with a short buzz. Press to Copy or hold to dismiss. Idle screen shows time and room conditions.

## Charging
Place the polished side down on any Qi pad. Charge time about 1.5 hours.

## Camera
The camera captures only when you approve on the device. The screen shows an indicator while it is active.

## Care and safety
Do not open the case. Do not use above 40 °C. Contains a lithium battery.

## Troubleshooting
| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| No notifications | Check Wi-Fi; re-pair |
| Won't charge | Center on pad; remove metal objects |
