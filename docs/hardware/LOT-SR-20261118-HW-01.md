## 4. Key findings (the analysis)

1. **R1: a solid steel back cannot charge wirelessly.** Qi heats the steel and its foreign-object detection stops charging. Recommendation D1-B: a Ø22 mm mirror black zirconia disc in the polished back, plus a magnetic pogo dock in the box (this dock is requirement #12, "Charger").
2. **R2: the steel box weakens Wi-Fi.** The antenna goes under the sapphire window strip, and the seam between the shells is isolated so it can work as a slot antenna. RSSI is measured at EVT.
3. **R3: at 5.0 mm there is 0 mm spare height** with a 110 mAh cell. Build EVT at 6.0 mm (180 mAh) and freeze the thickness after DVT.
4. **Battery life comes from the server choosing wake times (`next_wake_at`)**, not from polling. Model: about 15–20 days in Calm mode, still to be measured with a PPK2 in S2.
5. **Cost:** about $147–216 per unit landed; about $25–52k for the whole program to 100 shipped units. Certification is the largest variable, and using a certified module saves about $10–15k.
6. **Timeline:** ship in August 2027. The critical path is the custom LiPo (30–45 days) and the D1 decision.
