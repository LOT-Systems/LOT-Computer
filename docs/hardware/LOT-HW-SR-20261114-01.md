## 4. Key findings (the analysis)

1. **Steel blocks Qi.** A solid polished-steel back (brief point 17) and wireless charging (point 19) conflict.
   Recommended fix: a Ø 26 mm NCVM mirror-silver glass window. It looks silver but is non-conductive. (D-1)
2. **5 mm is a stretch.** With off-the-shelf, pre-certified parts the stack is about 6.5 mm. 5.0 mm needs a chip-down ESP32-S3,
   a custom 1.6 mm cell and about $10k more FCC. Recommendation: build the first 100 at 6.5 mm. (D-2)
3. **Wi-Fi in a steel box** needs the antenna under the front glass. This is the top technical risk (R1).
4. **Server work is small and well-bounded.** It needs 2 tables, about 8 routes and 1 scheduled job, plus adding `device_copy` to `LogEvent` and to the `GET /api/logs` `displayableEvents` allow-list.
   Without that allow-list change, Copy entries would be saved but hidden from the Log tab.
5. **End users have no USB port.** Wi-Fi setup must use a SoftAP captive portal (added to docs, phase 2). `lotctl` is for the factory and developers.
