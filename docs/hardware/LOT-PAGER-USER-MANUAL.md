# LOT Pager — NODE-P
### User Manual (Draft v0.1 — pre-production)

*This manual describes the planned product as specced in the NODE-P
document set. It will be revised once PROTO-0 (LOT-PAGER-NODE-P-PLAN.md
§04, Phase P1) is in hand and the described behavior has been verified
against real hardware. Nothing in this manual should be treated as a
description of a shipped product.*

---

## 1. What it is

LOT Pager is a small stainless-steel device that sits on your desk and
shows you one thing at a time: a short message from lot-systems.com, like
*"Coffee time!"* It has one button, labeled **COPY**. That's the whole
product — a pager, not a phone.

## 2. In the box (planned)

- 1x LOT Pager (NODE-P)
- 1x Qi wireless charging stand
- 1x quick-start card

## 3. Setup

1. Place the pager on the charging stand to power it on.
2. On lot-systems.com, open **Settings → Devices → Pair a device**. A
   6-digit code will appear.
3. The pager will show a pairing prompt on its screen. Confirm the code
   matches what you see on the site.
4. Once paired, the pager is linked to your LOT account only. You can
   unpair it at any time from the same Devices screen — no need to touch
   the physical device.

## 4. Using it

**When a message arrives:** the screen lights up with one line of text.

**To acknowledge it:** press **COPY** (short press). The pager sends a
confirmation back to your Log tab on lot-systems.com, and the message
clears.

**To capture a moment:** press and hold **COPY** (about one second). The
pager takes one photo and attaches it to your current session — useful if
you want to give the LOT AI a bit more context about where you are or
what you're doing right now.

**Charging:** set the pager on its stand, polished side down. A full
charge from empty takes a few hours; topping up throughout the day keeps
it running indefinitely if it's usually docked when you're not at your
desk.

## 5. What it does *not* do

- It does not stream video or take photos continuously — only on a
  deliberate long-press of COPY, or if a specific prompt asks for one
  (you'll always see this on-screen before it happens).
- It does not replace notifications on your phone — it's a single,
  low-distraction surface for the one thing LOT thinks is worth a glance
  right now.
- It does not work without Wi-Fi and an active LOT account pairing.

## 6. Care

- Wipe the polished face with a soft cloth; avoid abrasive cleaners,
  which will dull the finish.
- The device is not rated for submersion. Light splashes are tolerated by
  the enclosure's gasket seal, but it is not a waterproof product.
- If the pager needs a factory reset, hold COPY for 10 seconds while on
  the charging stand. This unpairs it from your account.

## 7. Support

Questions or issues: refer back to your LOT account's Devices screen, or
the LOT Systems support channel listed on lot-systems.com.
