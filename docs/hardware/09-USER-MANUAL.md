# LOT Pager — User Manual (draft)

**Document:** LOT-HW-01 / MANUAL · **Rev:** A (2026-11-13) · Draft — final text after DVT

## In the box

LOT Pager · Wireless charging pad · USB-C cable · This guide

## Meet your LOT Pager

- **Back:** polished stainless steel.
- **Front:** screen, camera, and one button — **COPY**.

## 1. Charge

Place the Pager **screen-down** on the charging pad. The screen shows `CHARGING` and the
battery level. A full charge takes about 1 hour and lasts about 2–3 weeks.

## 2. Connect to LOT

1. On a computer, sign in at **lot-systems.com** → Settings → Devices → **Add LOT Pager**.
2. Press and hold **COPY** for 5 seconds until the screen shows `SETUP`.
3. Follow the steps on the website (choose your Wi-Fi). The screen shows `PAIRED ✓`.

## 3. Daily use

| You see / do | What happens |
|--------------|--------------|
| Two short buzzes | A new notice from LOT, e.g. "Coffee time!" |
| Press **COPY** | The notice and the local weather are saved to your **Log** on lot-systems.com |
| Hold **COPY** 1.5 s | Takes a photo and adds it to the Log entry |
| Press **COPY** twice | Shows temperature, humidity, and air quality around you |
| Pick it up | Shows the last notice |

## 4. Settings (on lot-systems.com)

How often notices come, quiet hours, buzz on/off, unpair.

## 5. Care

Wipe the polished side with a soft cloth. Splash-resistant, not waterproof. Don't heat above 45 °C.
Recycle as electronic waste — contains a lithium battery.
