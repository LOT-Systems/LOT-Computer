## Findings that change the brief
1. **5 mm is not achievable with camera + Qi + battery** (≈7.3 mm stack). Proposed: v1 at 7.5 mm, 5 mm as v2.
2. **Stainless blocks Wi-Fi and Qi.** The face needs a glass window over antenna and coil; device charges screen-down. The polished steel side stays solid.
3. **Camera + AI site = privacy-sensitive**: LED + opt-in, off by default.
4. **Brand and CQGS sources unread**: brand.lot-systems.com, lot-systems.com/about, institute.lot-systems.com/cqgs.html failed DNS from this sandbox. Finish/logo/typography rules are not applied.
