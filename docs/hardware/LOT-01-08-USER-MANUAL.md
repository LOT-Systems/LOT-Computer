## In the box

- LOT-01
- LOT Dock (wireless charger)
- USB-C cable
- This card

## The object

One side is mirror steel. The other side has a screen, a small camera and
one button: **COPY**.

LOT-01 shows short messages from your LOT account at lot-systems.com:
*"Coffee time."* · *"Rain at 16:00."* · *"Evening check-in."*
It buzzes twice when one arrives.

## First start

1. Put LOT-01 **face-down** on the LOT Dock. Connect the Dock to USB-C power.
   The screen shows *"Charging."*
2. On lot-systems.com open **Settings → Devices → Add**. You get a 6-digit code.
3. Hold COPY for 10 seconds. The screen shows *"Pair."*
4. On your phone, open the pairing page from the QR on this card. Choose
   your Wi-Fi, enter the 6-digit code.
5. The screen shows *"Paired."* Done.

## Every day

| You do                         | LOT-01 does                                           |
|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Nothing                        | Checks for messages every few minutes. Buzzes on new. |
| Press COPY                     | Sends the message on screen to your **Log** tab. Shows *"Copied to Log."* |
| Hold COPY (about 1 second)     | Same, plus one photo from the camera. Shows *"CAM"* first. |
| Pick it up                     | Shows time, temperature, humidity and air quality.    |

No network? COPY still works. LOT-01 saves up to 20 copies and sends them
the next time it is online.

## Charging

Face-down on the LOT Dock. A full charge takes about 2 hours and lasts about
3 days. The screen shows the battery level in the corner.

The mirror side does not charge. Only the screen side.

## Care

- Wipe the mirror side with a dry microfiber cloth.
- Do not cover the small slot on the bottom edge. That is where the
  weather sensor breathes.
- Not waterproof. Splashes are fine; do not submerge.

## Privacy

The camera takes a photo **only** when you hold COPY, and the screen shows
*"CAM"* first. Photos go only to your own LOT account. To remove the device:
**Settings → Devices → Remove**, then hold COPY 10 seconds to erase it.

## Reset

Hold COPY 10 seconds → *"Reset."* → the device forgets Wi-Fi and account.
