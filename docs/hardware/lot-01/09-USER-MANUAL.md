# LOT·01 — User Manual

**A small silver square that tells you one thing at a time.**

## In the box
LOT·01 · Wireless charging pad · USB-C cable · This card (scan the QR for the PDF manuals)

## Know your LOT·01
- **Mirror side:** polished stainless steel. Nothing to press.
- **Screen side:** the screen, the camera dot (top right) and the **Copy** key (bottom right).

## 1. Charge
Lay LOT·01 **screen-down** on the pad. When you lift it, the screen shows the battery level. A full charge takes about 1 hour and lasts about 2 weeks.

## 2. Connect to Wi-Fi
Press **Copy** once. The screen shows `Set up`. Open **lot-systems.com → Settings → Devices → Add LOT·01** on your phone and follow the steps (Bluetooth). Only 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi is supported.

## 3. Pair with your LOT account
The screen shows a code such as `LOT-7K2Q`. Enter it in **Settings → Devices**. You'll feel one short buzz and see `Paired.`

## 4. Everyday use
| You see / feel | What it means | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| Two short buzzes + a message, e.g. **"Coffee time!"** | LOT sent you a moment | Read it. Nothing else needed |
| — | | **Press Copy** → the message is saved to your **Log** tab with the time, place and room weather |
| One buzz + `Copied to Log` | Saved | |
| Three buzzes + `Copy failed` | No Wi-Fi or server | Try again later |
| Double-tap the case | Shows the last message, battery and room weather | |
| `Charge me ◐` | Under 10 % | Put it on the pad |

LOT sends at most 8 messages a day, and none at night (22:00–07:00).

## 5. Care
- Wipe the mirror side with a dry microfibre cloth. Stainless steel resists rust, but a mirror finish will show fine scratches over time.
- Water: splash-resistant only. Don't submerge it.
- Keep it between 0 °C and 40 °C. The weather sensor reads the room, so keep it out of direct sun for true readings.

## 6. Privacy
- The camera is **off** unless you hold Copy for 1.5 s (Phase 2 feature). A photo is only sent to your own Log.
- Remove a device at any time in **Settings → Devices → Remove**. This revokes its key immediately.

## 7. Specifications
40 × 40 × 5 mm · 316L stainless steel + glass · 1.28" 128×128 memory LCD · Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz, Bluetooth LE · Temperature, humidity, pressure and air-quality sensor · Motion sensor · VGA camera · 100 mAh Li-ion · Qi wireless charging

## 8. Safety & compliance
Contains a lithium-ion battery: don't heat it, crush it or open the case. Recycle according to local rules. FCC ID / CE marking: *to be assigned after certification (roadmap P5).*
