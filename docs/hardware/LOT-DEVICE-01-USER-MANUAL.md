# LOT-DEVICE-01 · User Manual

**Doc:** LOT-HW-MANUAL · Rev A (draft) · 2026-11-20

## In the box

- LOT Device (40 × 40 × 5 mm, stainless steel)
- Charger puck + USB-C cable
- Quick-start card

## The object

- **Front:** screen, camera, **Copy** button.
- **Back:** polished steel. To charge, place the device **face-down** on the puck.

## Setup

1. Charge the device face-down on the puck. The screen shows `LOT.`
2. On lot-systems.com, open **Settings → Device → Pair**.
3. Hold the device near your computer or phone and select **LOT-DEVICE** in the Bluetooth prompt.
4. Choose your Wi-Fi network. The device shows `Ready.`

## Daily use

- The device shows short notifications from LOT, for example `Coffee time.` It buzzes once when a new one arrives.
- **Press Copy** to send the message on screen to your **Log** tab. The device shows `Copied.`
- **Hold Copy** (phase 2) to take a photo and add it to your Log.
- The device checks for new messages about every 15 minutes. The screen keeps the last message visible while the device sleeps.

## Sensors

The device measures temperature, humidity, pressure and air quality. LOT uses these readings to time nudges. The device does not record audio.

## Battery

About 1 week per charge. A full charge takes about 2 hours. A low battery shows `Charge.`

## Remove a device

Go to **Settings → Device**, then **Remove**. The device stops receiving messages immediately.

## Care

Wipe the mirror back with a microfibre cloth. Keep the device dry: it is splash-resistant, not waterproof.

## Safety

Contains a lithium-polymer battery. Do not open, puncture or heat it. Use only the supplied charger. Regulatory statements (FCC/CE) will be added after certification (Roadmap S6).
