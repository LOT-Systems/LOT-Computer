## 5. MONK / MILITARY-COMMS PROPOSAL (design, not built)

Interactive widgets share one pattern worth standardising — a **SITREP line** posted to the Log on interaction:

```
SITREP 14:32 · 18°C CLR · ONLINE 12 · MEMORY ANSWERED · MOOD +1 · STREAK 9
```

Fields = weather store, time, `usersOnline`, widget event, delta. Rule: one line, fixed grammar, no free text — LOT® AI reads the Log as a transparent daily register (monk-ledger: what was done, when, in what conditions). Hook point: `useLogContext` + `logTriggers.ts`. Implementation deferred pending approval of the grammar.

## 6. 15-MONTH REVEAL & USAGE LOG

- Progression exists as `EvolutionState.featureUnlockLevel` (0–5), `$layoutDensity` (5 levels) and `MonthlyPulseWidget` (month counter from join date). There is no explicit month-1…15 schedule table; reveal is level-driven, not calendar-driven. Because of open #2 the month counter may be 0.
