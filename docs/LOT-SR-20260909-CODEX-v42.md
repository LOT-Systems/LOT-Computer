## v42 Additions — THE CONSOLE ROGUE (Word Turn Engine v32)

### Theme Rationale

The roguelike genre — beginning with Rogue (1980) and evolving through NetHack (1987), Spelunky (2008), Hades (2020) — provides the richest vocabulary for the self-care practice metaphor yet added to the LOT Badge Universe:

- **Permadeath**: endings that teach rather than punish
- **The run**: each practice session as a discrete, meaningful arc
- **Seeds**: intentions that shape the entire run
- **Meta-game**: the practitioner's accumulated knowledge across all runs
- **Boss rooms**: confrontations you cannot leave the floor without engaging
- **Artifacts**: what you carry forward between versions of yourself

This extends the existing RPG thread (Dungeon Map v20, Quantum Arcade v30) with the deepest game-design vocabulary for practice-as-play.

## Design Decisions

**Theme selection**: Console Rogue was chosen because v20 (Dungeon Map) and v30 (Quantum Arcade) exist but the roguelike genre's specific vocabulary — permadeath, seed, meta-game, artifacts between runs — had not been captured. This engine fills a genuine gap in the self-care metaphor library.

**Self-care resonance**: The roguelike metaphor maps cleanly to practice behavior:
- `permadeath` → therapeutic processing of loss and endings
- `run_start` → the fresh-start psychology of new practice periods
- `meta_run` → the reflective capacity to see one's own history as a pattern
- `boss_room` → naming and engaging with what cannot be avoided
- `combo_break` → non-catastrophic relationship with broken streaks
- `the_seed` → circadian consistency as seed-setting

**Secret bosses**: NetHack (MYTHIC) is the deepest easter egg because it requires genuine knowledge of roguelike history. Hades (EPIC) rewards players who connect the literary roguelike to their practice. Original Rogue (RARE) is accessible to anyone familiar with the modern roguelike scene.
