The Dreamscape engine was designed around the intersection of:
- **Jungian psychology** — archetypes, shadow work, individuation
- **Sleep science** — hypnagogia, circadian rhythm, REM
- **Dream journaling** — the original self-care writing practice
- **Sci-Fi / Fantasy** — dreamscape as simulation, lucid dreaming as hacking

The Secret Boss tier for v23 specifically honors:
- **Carl Jung** — mapping the inner world (collective unconscious, individuation)
- **August Kekulé** — whose dream of a snake eating its tail solved the benzene ring structure (science via dream)
- **Lucid dreaming** — "I am dreaming" as a mythic phrase that collapses the observer/dream boundary

These connect to LOT's core theme: the inner life as the primary RPG.
