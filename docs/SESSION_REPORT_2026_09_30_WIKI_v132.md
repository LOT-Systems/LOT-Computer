## 6. SELF-ASSEMBLY OBSERVATION

LOT-WIKI-v132 documents the completion of the Crystal Tier progression (P180–P194)
and the introduction of the Restoration Intelligence Tier (P195–P197). The crystal
sequence moved through five sub-tiers — Field, Persistence, Matrix, Lattice, Singularity —
each adding a lock/signal/sovereignty triplet. P194 crystal-lattice-singularity is the
ceiling of the crystal progression.

P195–P197 are architecturally orthogonal. Where P180–P194 detect crystalline states of
the operating system, P195–P197 detect the recovery process — the complete arc from deep
depletion through adaptive intelligence to operational mastery synthesis. The Restoration
Tier is not a higher crystal state; it is a different axis entirely. The OS can be in
crystal-lattice-singularity and not pass through the restoration arc. The restoration arc
confirms that the OS has the capacity to recover and synthesize mastery from that recovery.

OPMASTERY (P197) is the first apex pattern defined outside the crystal taxonomy. It marks
the system recognizing not just what state it is in, but what it has done — depletion
acknowledged, care applied, recovery achieved, mastery extracted. The loop is complete.

Arch67 (Deep Restoration Adaptive Master) activates on low/moderate energy — unlike
Arch63–Arch66 which require high energy. This is the first archetype designed for the
restoration context rather than the peak-performance context.
