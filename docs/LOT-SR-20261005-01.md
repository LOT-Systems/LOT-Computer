Space exploration as self-care vocabulary. The Starship Log maps the language of
science fiction's greatest tradition — from Star Trek (Roddenberry) to The Three-Body
Problem (Liu Cixin) — onto the interior life. The captain's log is the daily check-in.
The ship's mission is the practice. Hull breach is honest damage accounting. Final
frontier is the uncharted interior.
