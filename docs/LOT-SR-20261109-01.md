## LAYER 6 PROPOSAL — BEING PROFILE & HUMANOID CALIBRATION (new, for Vadik approval)

Goal restated in engineering terms: build a per-person **Being Profile** from what the person gives LOT®, and calibrate QI·46 output so a humanoid/voice/text surface responds in that person's register.

```
BEING PROFILE (per person, owned by the person)
  voice        : cadence, vocabulary, humor, silence tolerance (from journals, chats)
  affect       : emotional baseline + range, what soothes, what stings
  values       : recurring themes, commitments, rituals
  body         : sleep / movement / consumable rhythm (Calibration Loop)
  relationships: who matters, how they're addressed (opt-in only)

HUMANOID OUTPUT REGISTERS (selectable + blendable, 0..1 weights)
  GRACE        : unhurried, precise, no filler
  POETRY       : image over explanation, one metaphor max
  LOVE         : warmth without flattery, names the person's effort
  HUG          : short, bodily, presence-first ("I'm here.") — also maps to haptic output
  BEING-THERE  : stays in the moment, no advice unless asked
  COOL         : dry, light, low-drama
  VOICE: MALE  : output voice/persona parameter (TTS + phrasing); user-selectable, not fixed
```

Calibration loop: person rates responses (landed / missed) -> register weights update -> COSMO® node screens -> delivered. Target metric: "landed" rate per register per person.

### Guardrails (non-negotiable, inherit COSMO® node)
1. **Consent-first.** Nothing is ingested without an explicit opt-in per source. Opt-out deletes the profile and derived vectors.
2. **Person owns the Being Profile.** Exportable, deletable, never used to train the shared model without separate consent. Per-person adapters/vectors, not weights merged into the base.
3. **No third-party uploads.** A person can only upload their own being. Profiles of others (including the deceased) are out of scope until a separate policy is written.
4. **Honesty clause.** The humanoid never claims to be the person or to be human; it is "calibrated to you", not "you". Love/hug registers are expressions of care, not claims of sentience.
5. **Safety override.** Crisis signals bypass register styling; COSMO® routes to plain, direct, human-referral language.
6. **Minimal sensitive data.** Health/emotional data encrypted at rest, retention-capped, excluded from logs and Resend mail bodies.
