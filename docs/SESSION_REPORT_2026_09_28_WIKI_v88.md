### 2b. Word Turn v23 — Badge Design Rationale

**critical_hit** — The natural 20. The mechanic that transcends the game.
Detecting this word acknowledges that something exceptional happened in
the session, and it is logged as a RARE badge.

**level_up** — The core RPG progression unit. When someone writes "level up"
they mean genuine advancement. The badge acknowledges the milestone.

**respawn** — Recovery and resilience metaphor. Writing "respawn" or "extra life"
is the language of return from difficulty, making it a self-care trigger.

**health_potion** — The most direct self-care metaphor in the RPG vocabulary.
Rest is a health potion. Sleep is a health potion. The badge fires on any
self-identified restorative act.

**save_point** — The checkpoint metaphor. "Progress saved" is what the journal IS.
Every entry is a save point. COMMON rarity because it should be caught often.

**dungeon_cleared** — The room/day metaphor. The dungeon is the day. Cleared
means the hard parts were walked through. RARE because it requires actually
naming the difficulty as a dungeon.

**boss_defeated** — EPIC rarity. Writing "boss" or "final boss" in a journal
means a significant challenge was named and overcome. Deserves the weight.

**turing_play** [MYTHIC SECRET BOSS] — The Turing Test sits at the intersection
of RPG (the imitation game) and computer science. Writing Alan Turing's name
or the Turing test in a self-care journal is a MYTHIC-tier intellectual easter egg.

## 6. DESIGN DOCTRINE — THE DUNGEON MASTER LAYER

The v23 Dungeon Master system establishes the RPG vocabulary layer as the third
in a trilogy of game-culture engines:

- v14 / v17 — **The Neon Arcade** — retro video game aesthetic (pixel/sprite/8-bit)
- v18 / v21 — **The Cyberspace Codex** — sci-fi concept vocabulary (matrix/cyberspace/grok)
- v20 / v23 — **The Dungeon Master** — tabletop RPG mechanics (level_up/save_point/boss)

The progression is cultural: arcade games → cyberpunk fiction → tabletop RPGs.
Each vocabulary is a different era's self-understanding through play.

The Dungeon Master layer has the broadest self-care integration because RPG
mechanics were designed as explicit progress systems. "Hit points" is an
abstraction of vitality. "Save point" is an abstraction of memory and continuity.
"Level up" is the clearest possible metaphor for growth. The LOT terminal
receives these as the genuine signals they were built to be.

**The key design insight of v23:** the RPG invented the vocabulary of the
quantified self before the quantified self existed. D&D (1974) had stats,
experience points, health tracking, and character advancement before any
wearable device was built. The LOT system recognizes this vocabulary because
it is the natural ancestor of the LOT system's own design.
