## 3. Key engineering findings

1. **Stainless steel blocks Qi and Wi-Fi.** The coil and antenna can't go behind the polished back. Both sit under the front glass. The pogo dock is a guaranteed fallback. (R-02)
2. **5.0 mm is at the edge.** Off-the-shelf parts stack to about 7.0 mm (EVT). About 5.5 mm is realistic for DVT. 5.0 mm needs a custom 1.5 mm cell + rigid-flex board. Recommendation: ship the 100 units at 5.5 mm. (R-01, D-02)
3. **Certification is the biggest cost.** Going from the thinnest SiP to a pre-certified module saves about $10–20k but adds height. (D-01)
4. **The site already has most of what's needed.** The `Log` model takes `device_copy` without schema changes, and `aiEngineManager` + scheduled jobs can write the nudges. What's missing is **device auth**, since the site only uses cookie sessions today. Drafted.
5. **AI-grade sensor set:** BME688 (weather + AI gas classes via Bosch AI Studio), BMI270 (on-chip gesture wake), HM0360 (on-sensor motion for TinyML camera).
