## In the box

* LOT SQ-1
* LOT Dock (wireless charger) + USB-C cable
* Microfibre pouch
* This manual

## Meet SQ-1

**Face A** (front) has the screen, the camera and the **COPY** button.
**Face B** (back) is polished stainless steel and carries your serial number.

SQ-1 is quiet. It shows one message at a time from LOT and buzzes twice when something new arrives. The screen keeps the message on display even while SQ-1 sleeps.

## 1. Charge

Place SQ-1 **face-down** in the LOT Dock, with the screen facing the Dock. The Dock's light breathes slowly while charging and goes still when SQ-1 is full (about 1 hour).

> Don't charge it with the steel side down on a phone charging pad. Steel doesn't pass wireless power and will get warm.

## 2. Pair with lot-systems.com

1. Sign in at **lot-systems.com** → **Settings → Devices → Add SQ-1**. Write down the 6-digit code.
2. Hold **COPY** for 10 seconds until the screen shows `LOT-SQ1-XXXX`.
3. Join that Wi-Fi network from your phone. A page opens. Enter your home Wi-Fi and the 6-digit code.
4. SQ-1 shows **Hello, <your name>**. You're done.

## 3. Everyday use

| You do | SQ-1 does |
|---|---|
| Nothing | Shows LOT's message of the moment, for example **"Coffee time!"** |
| Press **COPY** | Saves the message to your **Log** tab on lot-systems.com (✓ "copied to Log") |
| Hold **COPY** 1.5 s | Takes one photo (the small light is on while the camera is on) and saves it to your Log |
| Double-tap the case | Shows the room weather: temperature, humidity and pressure |

Night hours (23:00–07:00): SQ-1 doesn't buzz.

## 4. Weather & air

SQ-1 has an air sensor behind the small hole on its lower edge. Keep the hole uncovered. LOT uses the readings to time its messages, for example "Open a window for 3 minutes".

## 5. Privacy

* The camera takes a photo only when you hold COPY. It never records continuously.
* The privacy light is wired to the camera's power, so the camera can't run without the light being on.
* Photos and Log entries are visible only to you.
* To remove a device, go to **Settings → Devices → Remove**. SQ-1 forgets your account at once.

## 6. Care

* The steel back is mirror-polished. It will collect fine marks over time, which is normal for polished steel. Wipe it with the pouch.
* Splash resistant (IP54 target). Don't submerge it.
* Operating temperature: 0–40 °C.

## 7. Battery

About 8 days per charge with default settings. LOT adjusts how often SQ-1 checks for messages (more often in the morning, less at night). When the battery is low, SQ-1 checks less often.

## 8. Troubleshooting

| Screen shows | Do this |
|---|---|
| "Pair at lot-systems.com" | Pair again (section 2). The device may have been removed from Settings |
| "copy failed" | No Wi-Fi. SQ-1 will try again on its next check |
| Blank / faded | Charge on the Dock for 10 minutes |

## Safety & compliance

Contains a Li-ion polymer battery. Do not open, crush or heat it. FCC / CE statements will be added after certification (roadmap P6).
