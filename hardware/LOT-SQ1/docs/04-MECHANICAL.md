## 1. Two-part stainless body

| Part | Material | Process | Finish | Key features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Part 1: Front frame** (face A) | 316L | CNC from 6 mm plate | Fine bead-blast (silver satin), R0.8 edges | 37.6×32.2 glass pocket, Ø4.5 camera aperture, 5×3.5 key slot, BME688 vent Ø1.0 on the bottom edge, 4× M1.2 threaded bosses |
| **Part 2: Back plate** (face B) | 316L | CNC 0.4–0.5 mm skin + perimeter lip | **Mirror polish #8**, laser-etched LOT® mark + serial | 4× counter-bored screw seats, ferrite / battery datum |

* Outer envelope: **40.00 × 40.00 × 5.0 mm** (5.5 EVT), corner radius R3.0, steel wall 0.8 mm.
* Joint: a 0.15 mm polyimide **isolator gasket** around the full seam keeps the two parts electrically separate (antenna slot + option-B charging contacts). IP54 target with a silicone bead.
* Mass estimate: 316L ≈ 8.0 g/cm³ → about 11–14 g for the shell, about 20 g total.

## 3. Z-stack (updated HW-02, generated by `tools/zstack.py --md`)

The case height is set by the **tallest column**, not by the sum of every part. In the *frame-board* layout, the PCB never sits under the battery: an 8 mm PCB leg runs beside the cell under the EPD, and the camera drops through a PCB cut-out in the strip below the screen.

| Layout | Column heights (mm) | Limiting column | Case Z (mm) | Verdict | VERIFY rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stacked (PCB under EPD) | A=4.50 / B=3.50 | A | **5.50** | 5.5 OK | 3 |
| frame board, 1.5 mm cell (EVT-2 target) | A=3.10 / B=1.70 / C=3.50 | C | **4.50** | 5.0 OK | 3 |
| frame board, 2.0 mm cell | A=3.60 / B=1.70 / C=3.50 | A | **4.60** | 5.0 OK | 3 |
| frame board, 1.5 mm cell, 2.8 mm camera | A=3.10 / B=1.70 / C=2.80 | A | **4.10** | 5.0 OK | 3 |

Walls (glass + OCA, back plate, tolerance) = 1.00 mm in every layout.

| Cell thickness | Cell footprint (mm) | Est. capacity | Est. life @15-min sync |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5 mm | 29 x 31 | ~80 mAh | ~9 days |
| 2.0 mm | 29 x 31 | ~107 mAh | ~11 days |

**Result:** a hard **5.0 mm** is reachable with the frame board and a **2.0 mm cell** (4.6 mm, leaving 0.4 mm margin), and the battery life is better than the HW-01 estimate. HW-01 assumed a stacked board; that layout is what forces 5.5 mm. **Recommendation for D1:** design EVT-2 for 5.0 mm (frame board), keep 5.5 mm only as the EVT-1 module-board fallback.

Three heights are still planning values (**VERIFY**): Qi coil + ferrite, Li-Po pouch, camera module. Replace them with datasheet values and re-run the tool before the layout freeze. Also check at EVT-1 that the EPD backplane does not detune the Qi coil sitting behind it.

## 4. LOT Dock (wireless charger)

* A CNC puck Ø60 × 12 mm with a **40.4 × 40.4 × 2 mm cradle** that locates the SQ-1 **face-down**, so the Qi coil lines up with the Tx coil through the glass.
* Inside: an off-the-shelf 5 W Qi Tx board, USB-C input, an LED ring hidden under a frosted rim (charging = slow breathe).
* Felt or TPU liner protects the glass. A magnet pair for alignment is optional, but keep it away from the coil (it would saturate the ferrite).
* Option B (contact Dock): two spring pins touch the front frame and the back plate.
