# 10 · LOT Pager L-01 · User manual

*Draft v0.1 · 2026-11-19*

## In the box

- LOT Pager L-01
- Wireless charging pad + USB-C cable
- Microfiber pouch
- This card

## Meet it

- **Back.** Polished steel. Nothing to press.
- **Front.** A small screen, a tiny camera and one button.

## Set up (2 minutes)

1. Charge it: place it **face down** on the pad. The screen says *Charging.*
2. On **lot-systems.com**, open **Settings → Devices → Add device**. You'll see a 6-digit code.
3. Press and hold the button for 2 seconds. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network **LOT-L01-xxxx** and enter your home Wi-Fi and the 6-digit code.
4. The screen says *Hello.* It's ready.

## Use it

- **Notifications.** LOT sends short notes when they matter, like *Coffee time.* The pager buzzes once and the note stays on the screen.
- **Copy.** Press the button once to save the note on screen to your **Log**. The screen says *Copied.* No signal? It remembers up to 20 presses and sends them later.
- **Weather and air.** The pager senses temperature, humidity, pressure and air quality so LOT can say things like *Air is stale. Open a window.*
- **Camera.** Off by default. When it's on, a small light shows in the camera hole. Nothing is recorded without that light.

## Battery

About 3 weeks per charge with normal use. Your battery level shows on **Settings → Devices**.

## Reset

Hold the button for 8 seconds while it wakes. The screen says *Reset.* and it's removed from your account.

## Care

Wipe the polished back with the microfiber pouch. Not waterproof: keep it away from water.

## Safety

Contains a lithium battery. Don't open, puncture or heat it. Charge only with a Qi pad or the LOT dock.
