## The object

TILE is a small steel square. One side is a mirror. The other side has a
silver screen, a camera, and one button: **Copy**.

lot-systems.com sends TILE short lines through the day: `Coffee time.`
`Window open, the air is fresh.` `Stand up and stretch.` When a line is worth
keeping, press **Copy**. It goes to your Log.

## Set up (3 minutes)

1. Put TILE face-down on its dock for 30 minutes before first use.
2. On lot-systems.com open **Settings → Devices → Add TILE**. A 6-digit code
   appears.
3. Hold **Copy** for 10 seconds. The screen says `Pair.`
4. Follow the steps on the site. It asks for your Wi-Fi and the code.
5. The screen says `Paired.` Your first line arrives within 15 minutes.

## The button

| Press              | What happens                               |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| Once               | **Copy.** The line goes to your Log, with the time, temperature and humidity. The screen says `Copied.` |
| Twice, quickly     | Next line                                  |
| Hold 2 seconds     | Take a photo. It goes to your Log. The screen says `Photo.` |
| Hold 10 seconds    | Pair again (new Wi-Fi or new account)      |

The camera never takes a photo unless you hold the button.

## The screen

```
  Coffee time.

  15:00  21C  44%
```

The top is the line from lot-systems.com. The bottom is the time, the room
temperature and the humidity, measured by TILE.

The screen is reflective. It needs no light of its own and stays on all the
time, like a printed card.

## Charging

Place TILE face-down (mirror up) in the dock. The screen shows
`Charging. 64%`, then `Charged.` A full charge takes about 2.5 hours and lasts
about two weeks.

## Care

- Wipe the mirror with a dry microfibre cloth.
- Splashes are fine. Do not submerge it.
- Keep the small hole on the top edge clear. It is how TILE senses the air.
- Do not open the case. There is a lithium battery inside.

## Privacy

- TILE sends only what it needs: your lines, your Copies, your photos (only
  when you hold the button), and room weather.
- Everything TILE writes appears in your Log, where you can delete it.
- Remove a TILE at any time from **Settings → Devices**.
