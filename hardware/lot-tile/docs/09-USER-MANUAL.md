# 09 · LOT TILE: User Manual

**Doc:** LOT-HW-01-UM · **Rev:** A (2026-11-14) · Applies to firmware 0.1.x

## In the box

LOT TILE · charging pad · USB-C cable · this manual

## Your LOT TILE

- **Front:** screen, camera, **Copy** button.
- **Back:** polished stainless steel. Place this side on the charging pad.

## 1. Charge

Place the tile, back down, on the pad. The screen shows `Charging. 64%`. A full charge takes about 1.5 hours.

## 2. Connect to Wi-Fi (first time)

1. The screen shows `Wi-Fi: LOT-TILE-4F2A`.
2. On your phone, join the Wi-Fi network **LOT-TILE-4F2A**. A setup page opens. If it does not, go to `http://192.168.4.1`.
3. Choose your home network and enter its password. The tile shows `Connected.` and then a pair code, `Pair: K7Q-2MX`.

## 3. Pair with lot-systems.com

1. Open **lot-systems.com → Settings → Devices**.
2. Enter the code from the screen.
3. The tile shows `Hello, <your name>.` and buzzes once. Done.

## 4. Daily use

| You see / feel | Meaning |
|----------------|---------|
| One buzz plus a line, e.g. `Coffee time!` | A notification from LOT |
| `09:41 · 12°C` under it | The time it arrived and the local temperature |
| `Offline.` | No Wi-Fi. The tile tries again on its next wake |

**Copy button**

| Action | Result |
|--------|--------|
| Press | Saves the line on screen to your **Log** on lot-systems.com. The screen shows `Copied.` |
| Press and hold (about 1 s) | Also takes a photo and attaches it to the same Log entry. The screen shows `Copied. Photo.` |
| Hold 10 s | Forgets the pairing. The screen shows `Forgotten.` and then a new pair code |

Pick the tile up to refresh the screen.

## 5. Quiet hours and frequency

Set these in **Settings → Devices** on lot-systems.com: quiet hours, maximum notifications per day, and buzz strength.
By default LOT sends at most 8 per day, at least 45 minutes apart, and none during your sleep window.

## 6. Care

- The back is mirror-polished 316L stainless steel. Clean it with a microfibre cloth and avoid abrasives.
- The tile is splash-resistant (IP54 target), not waterproof.
- Keep the small vent hole on the side clear. The weather sensor breathes through it.
- Do not charge it above 35 °C ambient. Use only Qi-compatible pads.

## 7. Privacy

- The camera is fully powered off except during a long press.
- Photos go only to your own Log. Sensor readings are temperature, humidity, pressure, air quality class and light level. No audio, location or motion data leaves the device.
- To revoke the tile, go to **Settings → Devices → Remove**. It stops working until it is paired again.
