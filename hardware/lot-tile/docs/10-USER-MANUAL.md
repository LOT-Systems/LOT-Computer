# LOT TILE — User Manual

*Firmware 0.1 · draft for the 100-unit edition*

## In the box
- LOT TILE (40 × 40 × 5 mm, stainless steel)
- LOT Dock (magnetic charger) + USB-C cable
- Quick-start card
- Soft pouch

## Meet your TILE

**Mirror side:** polished steel. Put it face-down on any Qi charging pad, with the black disc in the centre.
**Working side:** the screen, the camera (with its light), and one button: **COPY**.

## 1. Charge
Snap the TILE onto the LOT Dock, or lay it mirror-side down on a Qi pad. The screen shows `⚡ 64%`. A full charge takes about 2.5 hours.

## 2. Pair with lot-systems.com
1. Press the button. The TILE shows a **6-digit code**.
2. On lot-systems.com open **Settings → Devices → Add LOT TILE**.
3. Type the code, **or** hold the TILE up to the QR code on your screen. The camera light turns on while it scans.
4. Connect to Wi-Fi: the site shows a Wi-Fi QR code; hold the TILE to it.
5. The screen says `LOT · READY`.

## 3. Everyday use
- The TILE sleeps nearly all the time. When LOT has something for you ("Coffee time!"), it gives one soft buzz and shows the message. The message stays on screen without using power.
- **Press once — COPY.** The message is saved to your **Log** tab on lot-systems.com, marked `⧉ from LOT TILE`.
- **Press twice:** mark as read and see the previous message.
- **Hold 2 seconds — Snap** (only if you turned the camera on in Settings): one photo goes to your Log with the message. The light is always on while the camera is on.
- **Tap the case twice:** wake up and show local weather: temperature, humidity, air quality and UV.

## 4. Modes
- **Calm (default):** messages arrive at times LOT chooses for you. Battery lasts about 2 weeks.
- **Live:** messages arrive within seconds. Battery lasts about 1–2 days.
Change the mode on lot-systems.com → Settings → Devices.

## 5. Care
- The mirror side scratches like a watch case, and the marks become its patina. Wipe it with a microfibre cloth.
- The TILE resists rain and sweat (IPX4). Do not swim or shower with it.
- Charge it between 0 and 40 °C.

## 6. Reset
Hold the button for 10 seconds, until you feel three buzzes. This erases pairing and Wi-Fi.

## 7. Privacy
- The camera is off until you turn it on. While it is on, the light is on; that is wired in hardware.
- Sensor readings go only to your own LOT account.
- Remove the TILE from Settings → Devices to delete its data link.

## 8. Safety and compliance
Contains a lithium-ion battery (≈ 0.4 Wh). Do not open, puncture or heat it. Contains FCC ID: *see the label on the box*. Recycle as electronic waste.
