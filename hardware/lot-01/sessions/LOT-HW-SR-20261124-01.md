## 2. Key engineering findings

1. **A stainless body blocks radio and Qi charging.** The polished back stays solid. The front glass window is the only RF and power path, so the display, camera, Wi-Fi antenna and charging antenna all sit behind one sapphire panel.
2. **NFC wireless charging (Renesas PTX30W/PTX130W) is chosen over Qi.** It is designed for ring/earbud-size devices and avoids eddy heating in steel. Qi (TI BQ51003) is the fallback.
3. **5.0 mm is reachable on paper** with a split level: the battery sits beside the PCB, not under it. Zone A totals 4.20 mm and the worst Zone B column 3.90 mm. The camera module height (≤ 2.6 mm) is the deciding unknown.
4. **A bare ESP32-S3FH4R2 chip (not a module)** is needed for height. That means full FCC/CE certification (≈ $15–25k), which is the largest single cost.
5. **The Sharp LS013B7DH03 LCD shows 0 stock and a 28-week lead time** on Digi-Key. This is the critical-path purchase.
6. **Estimated battery life ≈ 8 days** on a ~90 mAh cell with 10-minute adaptive polling. Wi-Fi is more than 80 % of the power budget.
7. **The software path is short:** LOT already has `/api/contextual-prompts`, a flexible `logs` table and weather context. `device_copy` events will feed the existing weather-mood pattern analysis directly.
