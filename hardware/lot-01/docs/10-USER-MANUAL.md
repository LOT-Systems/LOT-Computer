# LOT-01 SQUARE — User Manual

## In the box

LOT-01 SQUARE · Charger puck · USB-C cable · This card

## 1. Charge

Place SQUARE **screen-down** on the puck. The puck light breathes while charging.
A full charge takes about 2 hours and lasts about a week.

## 2. Connect to LOT

1. On lot-systems.com go to **Settings → Devices → Pair**. A 6-digit code appears.
2. Hold **COPY** for 5 seconds. The screen shows `PAIR · LOT-01-XXXX`.
3. Click **Connect via Bluetooth**, pick `LOT-01-XXXX`, then enter your Wi-Fi and the code.
   *iPhone:* join the Wi-Fi network `LOT-01-XXXX` and enter the same details on the page that opens.
4. The screen shows `HELLO, <your name>`. You're done.

## 3. Use

* **A note arrives:** SQUARE buzzes and shows something like **"Coffee time!"**
* **Press COPY:** the note, the time and the local weather go to your **Log** tab.
* **Hold COPY (1 s):** takes a photo and adds it to the copy.
* **Double-press COPY:** dismisses the note.
* **Pick it up:** it shows the latest note.

## 4. Care

* The mirror side is polished 316L stainless steel. Wipe it with a microfibre cloth. Avoid abrasives.
* Splash-resistant (IP54). Do not submerge it.
* Keep the small hole on the bottom edge clear, because the weather sensor breathes through it.
* Operating temperature: 0–40 °C. Do not leave it on a hot dashboard.

## 5. Reset

* **Restart:** hold COPY for 10 seconds.
* **Unpair:** Settings → Devices → Revoke on lot-systems.com, then hold COPY 5 s to pair again.

## 6. Safety and compliance (to be completed after certification)

Contains a lithium-polymer battery. Do not open, puncture or heat it.
